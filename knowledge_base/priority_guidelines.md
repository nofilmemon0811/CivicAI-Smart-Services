
# Priority Guidelines

## Critical
Use when a complaint presents an immediate or potentially severe public safety, flooding, traffic, electrical or infrastructure risk.

## High
Use when the problem significantly affects residents, access, sanitation, safety or essential services and should receive prompt attention.

## Medium
Use for important civic maintenance issues that are not immediately dangerous.

## Low
Use for minor issues with limited immediate impact.

Priority is an AI-assisted estimate. Administrators should make the final operational decision.

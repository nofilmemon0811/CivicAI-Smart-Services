
# Civic Department Responsibilities

## Water & Drainage
Handles water leaks, water supply problems, sewage, blocked drains, storm drains and drainage infrastructure.

## Roads & Infrastructure
Handles potholes, damaged road surfaces, sidewalks, road markings and related public infrastructure.

## Waste Management
Handles garbage collection, overflowing public bins, missed collection and waste disposal concerns.

## Electricity Services
Handles public streetlights and public electrical infrastructure. Electrical hazards should be treated as urgent.

## Public Safety
Handles reports about unsafe public spaces, damaged safety barriers and civic safety risks.

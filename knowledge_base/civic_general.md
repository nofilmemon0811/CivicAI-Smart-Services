# General Civic Services Guide

## Civic Complaints
Citizens can report public problems such as road damage, water leaks, drainage issues, overflowing waste bins, broken streetlights, unsafe public spaces, damaged sidewalks, blocked access and other municipal maintenance concerns.

A useful complaint normally includes the exact location, a clear description, when the problem started or was noticed, the visible impact, and a photo when safe and appropriate.

## Choosing a Responsible Service
The responsible service depends on the local government's structure. Common service areas include roads and infrastructure, water and drainage, waste management, electricity or street lighting, public safety, parks and recreation, public transport, environmental services and community services.

Department names vary between cities and countries. CivicAI should not invent a local department name when the user's location is unknown.

## Urgency
Issues involving immediate danger, fire, exposed electrical equipment, major flooding, serious structural damage, blocked emergency access or an active threat to life should be treated as urgent. CivicAI is not a replacement for local emergency services.

## Accessibility
Civic problems that block sidewalks, ramps, crossings, public entrances or other accessibility infrastructure should mention how access is affected and where the obstruction occurs.

## Public Transport
Public transport complaints may involve unsafe stops, damaged shelters, accessibility barriers, route information, service reliability or infrastructure. The correct authority varies by city.

## Environment and Parks
Common environmental or park complaints include illegal dumping, damaged public trees, unsafe park equipment, pollution concerns, blocked public spaces and maintenance issues. The responsible service varies by local government.

## Permits and Municipal Information
Questions about permits, fees, taxes, official forms, local laws, opening hours or government contact details are location-specific. CivicAI should ask for the city/country rather than inventing an answer.

## Real-Time Information
Current weather, live traffic, active outages, current public transport arrivals, current government announcements and other changing information require a live data source. A static knowledge base cannot reliably provide real-time information.
